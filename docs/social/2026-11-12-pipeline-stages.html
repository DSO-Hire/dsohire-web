The hiring playbook

A job ad is nota hiring process.

1
AttractA listing with real pay, real schedule, real expectations.
2
SourceSearch for candidates proactively instead of waiting for applications.
3
DecideStructured interviews, people making the calls, notes in one place.
4
CloseFast offers. Strong candidates have other options.
5
VerifyLicenses and credentials tracked before day one, not after.
Most groups have real tooling for stage one. The leak is in the other four.
