# Hiring software for dental groups (DSOs): sourced fact dossier

Prepared 2026-10-19 for four planned pages: "DSO Hire vs DentalPost", "DSO Hire vs Cloud Dentistry", "DSO Hire vs Indeed", and the roundup "Hiring software for dental groups (DSOs): the 2026 landscape".

Method: every fact below comes from a page fetched on 2026-10-19 (the "accessed" date unless a publication date is given). Quotes are copied from the fetched page text, 25 words or fewer. "Vendor" means the vendor describing itself; "Third-party" means press or an outside observer. Bracketed numbers point to the source list at the end. Anything we could not load and read ourselves is in "Rejected / unverifiable", not in the body.

---

## 1. Summary

- **The market splits into four published models.** (a) Dental job boards that charge a subscription per active post (DentalPost, $259/mo for one active post [5]). (b) Dental temp/perm marketplaces priced by subscription plus per-booking fees (Cloud Dentistry, "most popular" plan $97/mo [13]) or by a percentage service fee on temp shifts (Teero, 12% [17]). (c) General job boards that are free to post with optional pay-per-click sponsorship (Indeed [19], LinkedIn [22]). (d) General-purpose ATSs, either with public subscription prices (Breezy, Workable, JazzHR [25][26][27]) or quote-only (iCIMS, Paycor; Paylocity and UKG could not be loaded [28][29]).
- **DSO Hire's published model** is a flat monthly subscription per dental group, $399 to $2,999 per month (about 10% less billed annually), capped by active openings and admin seats, with no per-listing or placement fees and free for candidates [1][2][3]. It is permanent hiring only; it does not offer temp shift coverage.
- **Every dental competitor reviewed now says it serves DSOs.** DentalPost has a corporate recruiting page for "DSOs and multi-location groups" with volume packages on request [6]. Cloud Dentistry says it is "Trusted by 500+ DSOs" [11]. Teero has a "Staffing for DSOs" page with multi-location logins [16]. None of the three publishes DSO-tier prices; all say to contact them.
- **Permanent placement fees are not a differentiator against the dental marketplaces.** Cloud Dentistry [12] and Teero [17] both state they do not charge for permanent hires. Our comparison copy should not imply they do.
- **AI fit scoring is no longer unique.** DentalPost includes "candidate match scores" [5]; Indeed's Premium Plus (Beta) shows a "Smart Fit Score (0-100)" [19]; JazzHR Plus lists "AI-Powered Candidate Matching" [27]. DSO Hire's distinguishing claim is narrower: a two-sided, dentistry-specific fit model [2][3].
- **Large DSOs' own careers sites mostly run on enterprise ATSs.** On 2026-10-19 the careers pages of MB2 Dental, Smile Brands, Pacific Dental Services, and Gentle Dental pointed to iCIMS-hosted job pages; Aspen Dental's careers site loads Phenom [30]. That supports positioning DSO Hire as a sourcing and pipeline layer for mid-size groups, not a replacement for enterprise HRIS at the very largest DSOs.
- **No credible, published data was found on which channels DSOs use to advertise roles.** The only material found was vendor blog content. We recommend not making any "where DSOs advertise" claims (see Rejected).
- **Internal flag (DSO Hire's own pages):** /pricing and /for-dental-groups state that "sponsored listings on the major boards commonly run $200–$600 per posting", and /vs/staffing-agencies states agency fees of "15–25% of first-year salary". Neither page cites a source [1][2][4b]. Indeed's and LinkedIn's own pages describe pay-per-click budgets with no fixed per-post price [19][22], so the "$200–$600 per posting" figure should be sourced or softened before it appears on a page that names Indeed.

### 3.1 DSO Hire vs DentalPost

| Row | DSO Hire | DentalPost |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Dental-only hiring platform with built-in applicant tracking [3] | Dental-only job board; describes itself as the industry's "largest online and mobile job board" [5] |
| Pricing (as of 2026-10-19) | $399 to $2,999/mo per group; about 10% off annually [1] | $259/mo Job Posting Subscription; Resume Search +$149/mo; DSO volume packages by quote [5][6] |
| Active posts per price | 5 / 20 / 100 / unlimited active openings by tier [1] | "one active job post at a time" on the standard subscription [5] |
| Multi-location | Post once to many practices in a single flow, every tier [1] | Multi-location posting via corporate offering, priced on request [6] |
| Permanent hiring | Yes | Yes (full-time, part-time) [5] |
| Temp coverage | No | Temp job listings can be posted [5]; no shift-booking product described |
| Fit scoring | Two-sided dental fit model (PracticeFit) on every tier [2] | "candidate match scores" from assessment data (DISC, values, skills) [5] |
| Candidate cost | Free [3] | Not stated for applying; assessments free for job seekers [7] |
| Contract | Monthly or annual, cancel anytime [1] | Monthly auto-renew, cancel anytime [5] |

**When DentalPost is the better choice (honest):**
- You run one practice or have one role open at a time. $259/mo for a single active post costs less than our cheapest tier, and our own job-boards page says a one-off listing beats any subscription for occasional hiring [4a].
- You want proactive resume search into a large existing dental database today (Resume Search, "200K+ Resumes" claimed) [5].
- You already have an ATS and just need dental reach; DentalPost advertises integration with "220+ systems" [6].
- You value its personality and culture assessments as a screening input [5].

**FAQ (answerable from the facts):**
1. *How much does DentalPost cost?* As of 2026-10-19 its site lists a $259/month Job Posting Subscription with one active post at a time, plus an optional $149/month Resume Search add-on. Multi-location packages are quoted on request.
2. *Can I post for several locations on DentalPost?* Its corporate recruiting page offers multi-location posting for DSOs; pricing is via "volume packages" on request. DSO Hire includes multi-location posting on every tier.
3. *Do both score candidates?* Yes. DentalPost lists candidate match scores based on assessments. DSO Hire's PracticeFit scores two-sided fit between each practice's operating profile and the candidate's preferences, and never auto-rejects.
4. *Are there contracts?* Neither requires a long-term contract; both say you can cancel anytime.
5. *Do candidates pay?* DSO Hire is free for candidates. DentalPost says its assessments are free for job seekers; we found no candidate fee on its pages.

### 3.2 DSO Hire vs Cloud Dentistry

| Row | DSO Hire | Cloud Dentistry |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Permanent-hire platform with built-in ATS [3] | Dental staffing marketplace for temp, part-time, and permanent [10] |
| Pricing (as of 2026-10-19) | $399 to $2,999/mo per group [1] | Location- and term-based subscription; "$97/monthly" for a 12-month plan in popular markets; $20 completion fee per booking in some areas; optional Direct Pay $20 to $40 per booking [13][14][15] |
| Multi-location | Single-flow multi-location posting; per-location dashboards from Scale [1] | DSO dashboards, billing summary for all offices, unlimited admin users, account managers [11] |
| Permanent hiring | Yes | Yes, with no permanent placement fees [12] |
| Temp coverage | No | Yes: on-demand temp booking plus internal float pool [11][13] |
| Fit scoring | Two-sided dental fit model on every application [2] | Peer reviews and ratings ("95% of our professionals are rated 4+ stars") [11]; no fit score described |
| Candidate cost | Free [3] | Free to sign up for professionals [10] |
| Contract | Monthly or annual, cancel anytime [1] | 1, 6, or 12 month subscription terms [13] |

**When Cloud Dentistry is the better choice (honest):**
- You need same-week or same-day temp coverage for call-offs. DSO Hire does not book temps; Cloud Dentistry reports "<10 minutes for booking confirmation" [11].
- You want an internal float pool to share your own staff across offices; Cloud Dentistry says it is free for DSOs [11].
- Your hiring is mostly try-before-you-hire: it reports "6 out of 10" offices made a permanent hire after a temp [10].
- Budget is the deciding factor for a single office: its published price point ($97/mo on a 12-month plan in popular markets) is below our Solo tier [14].

**FAQ:**
1. *Is Cloud Dentistry a staffing agency?* It describes itself as a self-serve marketplace and says it does not charge placement, agency, or buyout fees for permanent hires made through the platform.
2. *What does Cloud Dentistry cost?* Subscription pricing depends on office location and term (1, 6, or 12 months). Its calculator cites $97/month for a 12-month plan in popular markets, plus a $20 completion fee per booking in some areas.
3. *Can I use both?* Yes. The models are complementary: Cloud Dentistry for temp coverage, DSO Hire for structured permanent hiring across locations.
4. *Which handles multi-location better?* Both offer multi-location tools. Cloud Dentistry focuses on staffing dashboards and float pools; DSO Hire focuses on posting one role to many practices and tracking each applicant through a pipeline.
5. *Do professionals pay?* Neither charges dental professionals to sign up, per each site.

4. *Can I use Indeed and DSO Hire together?* Yes; they are different layers (general reach vs dental pipeline). [Only claim job-feed syndication to Indeed if DSO Hire actually does it; not verified in this dossier.]
