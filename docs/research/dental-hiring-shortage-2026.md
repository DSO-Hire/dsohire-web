# The dental hiring shortage, by the numbers (2026): sourced fact dossier

Prepared 2026-10-19 for a DSO Hire research report. Audience: operators and recruiters at multi-location dental groups.

**How to use this file.** Every figure below was read on a page or PDF that was actually fetched on 2026-10-19 (PDFs were downloaded and text-extracted; chart values were checked against rendered chart images so each number maps to the right quarter or category). Each fact carries: the number, an exact quote (25 words or fewer), source, URL, date, and source type. Bracketed numbers like [1] point to the numbered source list at the bottom. Figures marked **DSO Hire calculation** are arithmetic we did on official BLS series; cite BLS plus "DSO Hire analysis" if used.

---

## Key findings

1. **About 9 in 10 dentists who recruit a hygienist say it is "very" or "extremely" challenging, and that has held for almost three years.** Q3 2026: 88.3% (n=704 private-practice dentists; asked only of those who recruited in the prior three months). The quarterly range from Q1 2024 to Q3 2026 is 87.7% to 91.7% [1].
2. **Only about 2 in 5 dentists who recruited a hygienist actually filled the role.** 42.1% in Q3 2026 vs 42.8% in Q3 2025. For dental assistants it was 66.8% vs 62.3% [1].
3. **One in five filled hygienist openings had been open for more than six months** (21.2% in Q3 2026, 21.5% in Q3 2025). For assistants, 7.5% (up from 4.5%) [1].
4. **4 in 10 dentists say they do not have enough hygienists.** Only 60.3% report an adequate number of hygienists, vs 73.5% for assistants and 79.3% for administrative staff (Q1 2026, n=796) [3].
5. **Staffing is a top-two challenge for 2026, and it ranks higher for DSO-affiliated dentists.** 54.2% of dentists named staffing as a top-3 challenge (second to insurance at 55.3%). Among DSO dentists, staffing ranked first at 59.3% [4].
6. **The federal government projects a 33,220 FTE hygienist shortage by 2038**, larger than its projected general-dentist shortage (19,860 FTEs) [17]. BLS projects about 15,000 hygienist and 53,000 dental assistant openings per year through 2035 [11][12].
7. **DSOs are where the next generation of dentists is going.** 16.1% of U.S. dentists were DSO-affiliated in 2024, up from 7.2% in 2015; 26.5% of dentists up to 10 years out of school are affiliated [9]. 32% of 2025 seniors heading into private practice intend to join a DSO [18].
8. **Dental office wages have not kept up with inflation since 2021.** Average hourly earnings of dental office production and nonsupervisory staff rose 24.6% from Jan 2021 to Aug 2026 while CPI rose 27.2% (BLS series; DSO Hire calculation) [15]. HPI's own Q3 2026 reading is 21% vs 27% [1].

HPI notes the panel skews toward ADA members, GPs, practice owners, solo or small-group dentists, mid-to-late career, white, and urban dentists [1]. ### Conflicts and caveats to disclose on the page

- **Wage growth since 2021:** HPI says staff earnings +21% (Q3 2026) and +23% (Q2 2026, through June). Current BLS series compute to about +24.6% through Aug 2026. The difference likely reflects data revisions, seasonal adjustment, or the index base. Either way wages trail CPI (+27%). Cite one consistently and name the series.
- **Hygiene enrollment growth:** 16% (HPI, 2020 to 2025) vs "nearly 20%" (ADA News, 2020-21 to 2024-25). Different windows.
- **Dentist supply outlook:** HPI projects dentists per capita *rising* through 2040 [9]; HRSA projects a 19,860 FTE general-dentist *shortage* in 2038 [17]. Different models. For DSO hiring, both agree hygienists are the tighter constraint.
- **Dentist headcount:** HPI 202,485 dentists (2024) vs BLS 161,400 dentist jobs (2025). Different definitions.
- **2022 capacity loss:** the same HPI report says 10% (summary) and 11% (body).
- **Recruiting-difficulty denominators** are dentists who recruited in the prior three months (roughly one-third of respondents), not all dentists.
- **HPI panel skew** toward owners, solo/small-group, and ADA members; DSO-specific subgroups are small.
- **DANB 2026** survey response rate is 3%; **GoTu** is a vendor convenience sample.
