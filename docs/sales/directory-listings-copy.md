## Platform steps

### 1. Crunchbase (15 minutes, do first)

1. Create a free account at crunchbase.com with cam@dsohire.com.
2. Search "DSO Hire" first to make sure no profile already exists. If one does, choose **Edit** to claim it instead of creating a duplicate.
3. If none: use **Add New** (or "Submit a new organization"), then Organization.
4. Fill in from Core facts: name, website, short description, HQ, founded year, founder (add yourself as a Person and link as Founder), categories (Recruiting, Human Resources, SaaS, Health Care), LinkedIn URL, employee range, contact email.
5. Submit. Crunchbase reviews edits; it can take a few days to go live.

### 2. Capterra (+ GetApp + Software Advice in one go, about 20 minutes)

Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice are all Gartner Digital Markets; one free vendor listing covers all three.

1. Go to vendors.capterra.com and choose **Get listed** / **List your product for free**.
2. Sign up with cam@dsohire.com, and verify the email.
3. Add the product: paste the name, website, short and long descriptions, categories, features, pricing (starting price $399/month, "flat rate", "per month"), deployment (cloud), and target market.
4. Upload the logo and 3–5 screenshots. Use clean ones from the live demo: the dashboard, a job's pipeline, a candidate with a PracticeFit score, and the analytics page. Keep the "sample data" feel; don't show anything that looks like a real person's private details.
5. Submit for review. Gartner checks the product is live and real, which can take a week or more. Choose the **free** listing; skip paid (pay-per-click) options for now.

### 3. G2 (about 20 minutes)

1. Go to sell.g2.com, then **Get a free profile** (or "Claim your profile" if G2 auto-created one; search for DSO Hire on g2.com first).
2. Sign up with cam@dsohire.com, then add the product with the same descriptions, categories (Applicant Tracking Systems, Recruiting Software), features and pricing.
3. Upload the logo and the same screenshots.
4. Submit. G2 shows the profile, but you won't appear in category rankings until you have reviews.

## Reviews: the rules

The listings matter now (links, and facts AI engines can read). Reviews are what make them rank, and they have to be real.

- **Only real customers or real users** may review. Never write reviews yourself, have friends post them, or pay for positive ones. G2 and Capterra both detect and remove fake reviews, and it can get the listing pulled. The FTC also treats fake reviews as deceptive.
- **Gift cards for honest reviews are allowed by both platforms** when given for any review, positive or negative. Use their official review-campaign tools, not your own.
- **Ask your first customers 30–60 days in,** once they've used it, using the platforms' own review links.
- **Reply to every review,** good and bad.
